Important: the head is geometrically protected against the active object (engine/pillar/wheel/pole/tree/wall). Engine and wheels only protect with the officer crouched — pillars, pole, tree and wall protect even standing. The torso is always considered protected by a ballistic vest, regardless of geometry. Visual result, not a ballistic certification.
Threat caliber (Handgun / Rifle, adjustable per threat in the list below): changes which objects count as cover, based on the 1st Ballistic Impact Test of the 20th BPM/M (AGSP, Brazilian Army, 10/31/2017):
• Engine — resists handgun and rifle (tested; no caliber went through, rifle only perforated the casing).
• Tree — resists rifle (tested, even sustained fire); handgun not tested, treated as resistant by inference.
• Pole — resists rifle on a single shot, but fails under saturation (3 rounds of 7.62 or 6 of 5.56 at the same point — the tool doesn't model this, so treat it as "short-term" cover against rifle); handgun not tested, treated as resistant by inference.
• Block wall — resists handgun, but doesn't resist rifle (tested: 1 shot went through).
• Pillar — not tested; the user's hypothesis (pending field validation) is that it only protects against handguns. Rifle is already treated as capable of penetrating it.
• Wheel — not tested; field tests pending. For now it counts as cover for both calibers, until real data exists.
